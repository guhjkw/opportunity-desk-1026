    The question in your post

    How two managers can never claim the same opportunity.

    Two people tapping Claim in the same second is the moment a simple automation breaks. This is how the build prevents it, and the demo above runs the same rule.

    What goes wrong in a simple build

    1. Maya and Priya tap Claim 31 ms apart.
    2. Make starts two runs at the same time.
    3. Both runs read the record: "Unclaimed".
    4. Both write their name. Both get "it's yours". One of them is wrong.

    What this build does

    1. The Claim link calls one Make scenario with sequential processing on, so requests are handled one at a time, in the order they arrive.
    2. Each run re-reads the Airtable record and only writes if it is still Unclaimed, bumping a version number.
    3. The second run sees "Claimed by Maya" and replies "already claimed by Maya at 09:00".
    4. Everyone else is told it is taken, so nobody calls the client twice.
    How it works

    Four steps. One of them is yours.

    Staff keep forwarding inquiries the way they do today. Everything after that runs on its own and tells you when it needs you.

    01 · YOU

    Forward the inquiry

    Anyone at the company forwards a new project email to one central address. That is the only change for staff.

    02

    Record and notify

    The email is read, an opportunity is created in Airtable, and every authorized account manager gets a Claim link. Existing customers go to their own manager.

    03

    Claim or rotate

    First valid claim wins. After 4 business hours everyone is reminded; after 1 business day the rotation assigns it.

    04

    Chase and report

    First contact is due within 1 business day, then reminded and escalated. Every Friday the team gets the shared report.

    Scenario 1 · Intake

    instant, on every forwarded email
    Gmail or Outlook: new email→AI step: name, contact, project, town, budget→Airtable: duplicate and customer check→Airtable: create opportunity→Email: Claim link to managers

    Missing details are flagged on the record instead of guessed.

    Scenario 2 · Claim

    instant, sequential processing on
    Webhook: Claim link→Airtable: re-read record→Still unclaimed?→Airtable: owner, time, version→Email: confirm, tell the others

    The authorized list is an Airtable table, so adding someone is one row, not a rebuild.

    Scenario 3 · Timers

    every 15 minutes, business hours
    Airtable: open opportunities→4-hour reminder→1-day rotation→Follow-up due, then escalate

    Business hours and the rotation order are settings in Airtable.

    Scenario 4 · Friday report

    Fridays, 4 pm
    Airtable: this week's records→Totals and per-manager table→Email: shared report link

    QuickBooks stays your estimating tool; the record keeps the estimate number and quote status.
